## A growing community, far from everywhere

Australia's Bangladeshi community has grown quickly, with the largest communities in Sydney and
Melbourne and growing ones in Brisbane, Perth, Canberra and Adelaide. Many arrived as students or
skilled workers and have built careers in healthcare, engineering, IT and business.

Finding a partner here has its own challenges:

- **Distance.** Australian cities are far apart, and Bangladesh is a long, expensive trip away.
- **Busy lives.** Long shifts, study and building a business leave little time to meet people.
- **A small circle.** In smaller cities, the community is warm but everyone knows everyone.
- **Family overseas.** Parents in Bangladesh naturally look for matches there, while many Bangladeshi
  Australians want someone who shares their life here.
