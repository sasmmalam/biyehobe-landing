## A large community, and still hard to meet the right person

British Bangladeshis are one of the most established Bangladeshi communities anywhere, with deep roots
in East London, especially Tower Hamlets, and strong communities in Birmingham, Oldham, Luton, Bradford,
Manchester and many other towns.

You might think that makes finding a partner easy. Many people find the opposite:

- **Everyone knows everyone.** In close-knit areas, families are often connected, and the circle can
  feel small and very public.
- **Introductions often come through relatives in Sylhet or Dhaka**, while you may want someone who
  shares your life in Britain.
- **Dating apps aren't built for marriage**, and big South Asian matrimony sites aren't built for us.
