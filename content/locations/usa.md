## A big country, a scattered community

The largest Bangladeshi American community is in New York City, especially Queens and the Bronx, with
strong communities in New Jersey, Michigan (including Hamtramck and Detroit), Texas, Georgia,
California and many other states.

That spread is part of the challenge. A great match for you may be two states away, and you may never
cross paths at a wedding or community event.

- **Family networks** are trusted, but often reach back to Bangladesh rather than across the US.
- **Community events** are warm, but you rarely know who is ready for marriage.
- **Dating apps** are built for casual dating, with few people who share our background.
