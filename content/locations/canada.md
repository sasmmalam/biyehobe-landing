## Singles everywhere, but how do you meet?

Toronto and the GTA are home to one of the largest Bangladeshi communities in North America, from the shops and
restaurants of Danforth East to families spread across Scarborough, Mississauga and beyond. There are
growing communities in Montreal, Calgary, Edmonton, Ottawa and Vancouver too.

Yet many Bangladeshi Canadians say the same thing: they know the right person is probably out there,
maybe in the same city. They just have no way of knowing who else is looking.

- **Family networks are trusted but small.** In a close community, everyone seems to be a cousin.
- **Community events are warm but awkward.** It is hard to know who is actually ready for marriage.
- **General dating apps aren't built for us.** Intentions vary widely, and very few members share our
  background.
- **Big South Asian matrimony sites are crowded.** Bangladeshis abroad are a small slice of their
  members.
